================================================================================
                    📞 CALL SCRIPTS - OUTREACH APPROACH
================================================================================

🎯 CALL STRATEGY OVERVIEW:
   1. First Contact: Text message (less intrusive, allows them to respond when ready)
   2. Follow-up: Phone call within 24-48 hours if no response
   3. Second Follow-up: Final text/call after 5 days

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📱 INITIAL TEXT MESSAGE TEMPLATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Hi [OWNER NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with [YOUR COMPANY]. I noticed your property 
at [ADDRESS] may be facing some tax challenges. I help homeowners in situations 
like yours explore their options - sometimes a quick sale can help resolve these 
issues and put money in your pocket. No pressure, just here to help if you want 
to talk. Would you be open to a brief conversation? Reply STOP to opt out."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📞 PHONE CALL SCRIPT - FIRST CONTACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

INTRODUCTION (15 seconds):
"Hi [OWNER NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with [YOUR COMPANY]. I hope I'm catching you 
at an okay time. I reached out via text earlier about your property on [ADDRESS]. 
Do you have just 2 minutes to talk?"

[IF YES - PROCEED]
[IF NO - SCHEDULE CALLBACK]

BUILD RAPPORT (30 seconds):
"I specialize in helping homeowners who are dealing with property tax challenges. 
I know these situations can be really stressful, and I want you to know there's 
no judgment here - I'm just here to help you understand your options."

IDENTIFY PAIN POINT (45 seconds):
"I can see from public records that there may be some outstanding property taxes. 
Can you help me understand your situation? Are you:
• Looking to keep the property but need help with the taxes?
• Considering selling to resolve the situation?
• Just exploring what options are available?"

[LISTEN - LET THEM TALK]

PRESENT SOLUTION (60 seconds):
"Based on what you're telling me, here's what I can offer:

[IF THEY WANT TO SELL]
"I can make you a fair cash offer on your property, as-is, and we can close in as 
little as 7-14 days. This would allow you to:
• Pay off the tax debt completely
• Walk away with cash in your pocket
• Avoid foreclosure and protect your credit
• Move on without the stress

The best part is, there are no realtor fees, no repairs needed, and no long 
waiting periods. Would you be interested in me taking a quick look at the 
property and giving you a no-obligation offer?"

[IF THEY'RE UNSURE]
"I completely understand - this is a big decision. What if I came by for just 
15 minutes to look at the property and give you a written offer? There's no 
pressure to accept, but at least you'll know exactly what your options are. 
You can take your time to think about it. Would that be helpful?"

CLOSE FOR APPOINTMENT:
"Great! I'm available [OFFER 2-3 TIME SLOTS]. Which works best for you?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📞 PHONE CALL SCRIPT - FOLLOW-UP (If they didn't respond to text)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Hi [OWNER NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with [YOUR COMPANY]. I sent you a text 
about your property on [ADDRESS] and wanted to follow up personally. I know 
dealing with property tax issues can be overwhelming, and I wanted to reach 
out because I may be able to help.

I'm not a realtor - I'm an investor who buys properties directly from homeowners, 
as-is, for cash. I can close quickly and help resolve tax situations like yours.

Do you have just a couple minutes to talk about your options?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📞 OBJECTION HANDLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OBJECTION: "I'm working with the city on a payment plan"
RESPONSE: "That's great that you're being proactive! Payment plans can definitely 
help. I'm curious though - even with a payment plan, would you consider selling if 
you could walk away with cash and be completely free of this situation? There's 
no harm in knowing your options."

OBJECTION: "I don't want to sell"
RESPONSE: "I completely understand - this is your home. My goal isn't to pressure 
you, but to make sure you know all your options. Sometimes homeowners don't realize 
that selling could actually put money in their pocket after paying off the taxes. 
Would it hurt to at least know what your property is worth in today's market?"

OBJECTION: "I need to think about it"
RESPONSE: "Of course, this is a big decision. Here's what I'll do - let me send 
you my contact information, and you can reach out whenever you're ready. But I 
also want to mention that with tax situations, time can be a factor. The sooner 
we can resolve this, the more options you'll have. Would you be open to me just 
coming by to give you a written offer to consider?"

OBJECTION: "How did you get my number?"
RESPONSE: "Your contact information is available through public property records. 
I know it can feel unexpected to get a call like this, but I specialize in helping 
homeowners in challenging property situations. I only reach out because I genuinely 
believe I can help. Would you be open to hearing how?"

================================================================================
                    🔍 HOW TO FIND OWNER CONTACT INFO
================================================================================

Since these are public records, here's how to find owner contact information:

1. MASSACHUSETTS PROPERTY CHECKER
   Website: https://massachusetts.propertychecker.com/
   - Search by address
   - May include owner mailing address

2. HAMPDEN COUNTY REGISTRY OF DEEDS
   Website: https://massrods.com/hampden/
   - Search by property address or owner name
   - Find deed records with owner addresses

3. WHITE PAGES / PEOPLE SEARCH
   - Search owner name + city
   - Many services available online

4. SKIP TRACING SERVICES
   - BatchSkipTracing.com
   - SkipGenie.com
   - PropStream (propstream.com)

5. DIRECT MAIL IF PHONE UNAVAILABLE
   - Send postcard to property address
   - Send letter to owner's mailing address from deed records

================================================================================
                    ⚠️ LEGAL & ETHICAL GUIDELINES
================================================================================

DO:
✅ Be respectful and compassionate
✅ Be honest about your intentions
✅ Provide value and information
✅ Follow up appropriately (max 3 contacts)
✅ Honor opt-out requests immediately
✅ Get everything in writing
✅ Consult with a real estate attorney

DON'T:
❌ Pressure or harass owners
❌ Make false promises
❌ Contact outside reasonable hours (8am-8pm)
❌ Contact after they say they're not interested
❌ Misrepresent yourself or your services
❌ Ignore TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act) rules

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES TO MAKE:
• "I'm a real estate investor, not a realtor"
• "I may make a profit if I purchase your property"
• "You should consult with an attorney or financial advisor"
• "You have the right to seek other offers"
